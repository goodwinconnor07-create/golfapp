Common questions

Do my friends need to buy the app?
No. The host starts the game in the app. Everyone else joins free with the link or code, in the app or on the website.

How do I add someone who doesn't have their phone?
In the lobby, tap No phone? Add them here. You enter their picks and scores on your phone.

Someone's phone died mid-round.
The host can enter scores for anyone. Open the waiting room and tap Enter for them.

A score is wrong.
Fix your own until the group moves on. After that, tap the score on the scorecard to ask for a change. The player (or the host) approves it, and the change shows on the scorecard for everyone.

There's no signal on the course.
Keep playing. Scores are saved on your phone and sync when you're back in signal.

Someone joined with a name that isn't OK.
The host can remove players from the lobby. Rude names are blocked, but no filter catches everything, so let us know and we'll add it.

How does scoring work?
Your golf score against par, plus your card: complete it and strokes come off, fail it and they go on. Lowest total wins. Open How to play in the app for the full rundown.
