CARDSHOT

Privacy policy

Last updated 15 October 2026

The short version: no accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics. We keep only what's needed to run your game, and online games are deleted 14 days after they were last played.

What CardShot stores

  • Player names and pictures you type in or pick (an emoji or a letter on a colour). Nicknames are fine.
  • The game itself: the course name, pars, the cards drawn, strokes, results, votes and any score changes.
  • A random ID for your phone, made by the app, so it knows which players are yours. It isn't tied to your name, email or Apple ID.
  • A notification token, only if you allow notifications in the iPhone app, so we can tell you when it's your turn or results are in.

We don't ask for your email, phone number, location, contacts, photos or payment details. If you buy the app, Apple handles the payment and we never see your card.

Where it's kept

  • On your phone: your name and picture, the game in progress and your past games. They stay there until you delete the app (or clear the website's data).
  • On our server (Cloudflare), only for games played online across several phones, so every phone sees the same game. Each online game is deleted automatically 14 days after its last change, notification tokens included.

Like any website, Cloudflare briefly keeps basic request logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect the service. We don't use them to identify anyone.

Who else sees it

  • The other players in your game see the names, pictures and scores in it. That's the point of the game.
  • Course search: when you look up a course, the words you type are sent to GolfCourseAPI and OpenGolfAPI to find it and its pars. Nothing about you or your game is sent.
  • Notifications go through Apple's push notification service.

We don't sell or share data with anyone else, and nothing is used for advertising.

Deleting your data

Delete the app (or clear the website's data in your browser) to remove everything on your phone. Online games delete themselves after 14 days. If you want one removed sooner, email us with the game code.

Children

CardShot is a family-friendly golf game. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children, and players can use nicknames.
